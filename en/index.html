The house

An honest table,
in the heart of the 6th.

A former Marseille storefront turned into a whitewashed dining room — raw-oak tables, local ceramics, slanted daylight. Around thirty seats, no more: the real luxury is being expected.

On the menu: line-caught fish, Provençal vegetables, and a living cellar sourced from growers we know and trust. Short, clear, honest.
