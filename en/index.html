ROSE brings the Mediterranean into a quiet, light-filled room in the city’s 6th arrondissement. Clean plates, a living cellar, thoughtful service.

The room

Rue Louis Maurel,
steps from Cours Julien.

A former Marseille storefront turned into a whitewashed dining room — raw-oak tables, local ceramics. Midday light comes in slant; evening light settles quietly.

Around thirty seats, no more: real service, familiar faces, the pleasure of being expected.

The kitchen

Produce first,
gesture second.

Line-caught fish, Provençal vegetables, olive oil from the hills around Marseille. A short menu, rewritten with each delivery, that lets the produce lead.

On the wine side, living bottles from growers we know — often with no added sulphur, always chosen with intent.

Cooking Marseille means cooking encounter — between the sea and the hills, between here and elsewhere.
— The house
